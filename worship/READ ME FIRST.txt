OnStage Worship
===============

Church presentation software with an AI assistant that listens to the
preacher and shows Bible verses on screen automatically.


INSTALL ON WINDOWS (10 or 11)
-----------------------------
1. Right-click the downloaded zip file and choose "Extract All...".
   (Don't run anything from inside the zip - extract it first.)
   Put the folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Start OnStage Worship.bat".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and
     then "Run anyway". This happens because the program is new and not
     signed. It only needs doing once.
3. OnStage Worship opens in its own Microsoft Edge window. A shortcut
   called "OnStage Worship" is added to your Desktop. Use that next time.

If the .bat file still won't open (some computers block .bat files):
   right-click "index.html" > Open with > Microsoft Edge (or Google Chrome).
   That runs the exact same app.


INSTALL ON MAC
--------------
1. Double-click the zip file to unpack it.
2. Right-click "index.html" > Open With > Google Chrome.
   (Install Chrome first if needed: https://www.google.com/chrome/)

   Or double-click "Start OnStage Worship (Mac).command" to open it in its
   own window. If macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "Apple could not
   verify" it: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and
   click "Open Anyway" next to the message. Then double-click it again.


USING IT ON SUNDAY
------------------
1. Connect the projector or TV as a second screen.
2. Click "Output" at the top. Drag the new window onto the projector,
   then double-click it to make it full screen.
3. Build your service in the Schedule using the tabs at the bottom
   (Scripture, Songs, Media, Presentations).
4. Click "Start listening" in the AI Verse Assistant panel and point the
   microphone at the preacher, or plug in an audio feed from the sound desk.
   When a verse is mentioned, it goes on screen by itself.
   Choose "Auto preview" or "Suggest only" if you prefer to approve each
   verse before it shows.
5. Press the "?" button in the app for all keyboard shortcuts.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- The AI microphone feature needs an internet connection. The browser's
  speech service turns speech into text. Everything else works offline.
- Your songs, slides and settings are saved on this computer automatically.
  Use Settings > "Export everything" to make a backup.
- Five Bible translations are built in, all free to use:
    KJV  - King James Version
    BSB  - Berean Standard Bible (modern English, like NIV/ESV in style)
    NHEB - New Heart English Bible (modern English)
    ASV  - American Standard Version 1901
    BBE  - Bible in Basic English (simple English)
  Choose one in the Scripture tab. The AI uses whichever is selected.
  NIV, ESV, NKJV, NLT and NASB are copyrighted, so they can't be included.
  If your church holds a licence for one of them in a digital (JSON)
  format, import it from Settings.
- 30 classic public-domain hymns are built in (Amazing Grace, It Is Well,
  Great Is Thy Faithfulness, Holy Holy Holy, Blessed Assurance and more).
- Modern worship songs are copyrighted. To add them, use your church's
  CCLI licence: in the Songs tab click "SongSelect (CCLI)", find the song,
  download the lyrics (.txt), then click Import or drag the file onto the
  song list. ChordPro (.cho), OpenLyrics (.xml) and plain text files can
  also be imported.

IF THE AI DOESN'T HEAR ANYTHING
-------------------------------
Click "Mic check" in the AI Verse Assistant panel. It tests each step and
tells you what to fix. The usual causes are:

- The app opened in Safari, Firefox or another browser. Open it in
  Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (right-click index.html > Open with).
- The wrong browser. Only Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge include the
  speech service. Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Firefox and Chromium do not.
- The microphone is blocked. Click the padlock/settings icon next to the
  address (or the ... menu in the app window), choose Site permissions,
  and set Microphone to Allow. On Windows also check: Settings > Privacy &
  security > Microphone > "Let desktop apps access your microphone" = On.
- The wrong microphone. The app uses the computer's DEFAULT microphone.
  Windows: Settings > System > Sound > Input. Mac: System Settings > Sound
  > Input. The level bar under "Start listening" moves when sound arrives.
- No internet. Speech recognition needs an internet connection.
- The microphone is busy in another program (Zoom, Teams, OBS). Close it.

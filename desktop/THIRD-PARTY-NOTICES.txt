OnStage Worship — third-party software and content
===================================================

OnStage Worship includes the following free software and content. Thank you
to everyone who made them available.

Speech recognition (runs offline on this computer)
--------------------------------------------------
* Whisper base.en speech recognition model — OpenAI, MIT License.
  https://github.com/openai/whisper
* sherpa-onnx (ONNX export of the model and the speech engine) — k2-fsa /
  Next-gen Kaldi, Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
* Silero VAD (voice activity detection) — silero-team, MIT License.
  https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
* ONNX Runtime — Microsoft, MIT License. https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

Application framework
---------------------
* Electron — MIT License (includes Chromium and Node.js; their licences are
  listed in LICENSES.chromium.html next to the program).

Bible texts (built in)
----------------------
* King James Version (1769) — public domain.
* Berean Standard Bible (BSB) — dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
* New Heart English Bible (NHEB) — public domain.
* American Standard Version (1901) — public domain.
* Bible in Basic English (BBE) — public domain.
  Text files from github.com/thiagobodruk/bible and github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases.

Hymns (built in)
----------------
* 30 hymn texts published in 1930 or earlier (public domain), from the
  public-domain entries of github.com/dsmithco/worship-chordpro.

Fonts
-----
* DM Sans, DM Mono, Montserrat, Oswald, Merriweather, Lora, Playfair Display —
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (packaged from Fontsource). Full licence texts are
  in fonts/FONTS-LICENSE.txt.
